# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

VoiceLK welcomes participation regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, religion, sexual orientation, language, education, or experience.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project events, and public spaces where someone represents VoiceLK.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, patient, and open to correction. Give specific, constructive feedback. Respect Sinhala and English speakers, dialect differences, accessibility needs, and different levels of technical experience. Credit others' work and respect privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, repeated disruption, unwanted contact, disclosure of private information, retaliation, and deceptive or abusive use of another person's voice are unacceptable.

## Reporting

For a repository comment, open its menu and choose **Report content** when available. If reporting to repository administrators is offered, use it to notify maintainers privately.

That option depends on GitHub repository settings and reporter eligibility. If unavailable, or if the concern involves a maintainer, use [GitHub's abuse reporting form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). GitHub handles those reports under its own policies; this is not a private inbox operated by VoiceLK. Do not post sensitive allegations in public issues.

Include relevant links, dates, and a concise explanation when safe to do so. Share only the personal information needed to understand the incident. For technical vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Repository administrators are responsible for reviewing reports they receive, considering context, and acting fairly. Responses may include a request to change behavior, a warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or a permanent ban. Severe incidents can lead directly to a ban.

Maintainers should limit sharing of report details to people who need them to respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. A maintainer involved in a report should recuse themselves and refer review to an uninvolved administrator where available.

A person affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through the private channel used for that decision. If no independent administrator is available, concerns about GitHub policy violations may be escalated to GitHub Support. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is prohibited.
